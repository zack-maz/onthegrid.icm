## Decisions Made

- **D-29 conservative-bias outcome accepted at face value: 0 `high-dead`.** No item cleared the bar of 'mechanically AND semantically dead with no plausible runtime path.' Every flag had at least one of: a documented public contract surface (CLAUDE.md, ADR-0005, etc.), a test-file consumer, a same-module-internal use that makes 'lose `export`' the right action (which is `probable-dead`-tier, not `high-dead`), or a Pitfall 1–5 false-positive pattern. **W4's deletion path therefore has zero bulk-approval throughput from W3** — every deletion candidate goes through individual review. This is the explicit intent of D-29; the wave validates the intent.
- **Lose-`export` introduced as a distinct W4 action class.** Of the 17 `probable-dead` items, 9 are lose-`export` narrowings (the symbol stays, only its visibility narrows from cross-file to module-private) and 8 are file/module-deletion candidates. W4 should commit lose-export changes as a separate atomic group from deletions because the blast-radius and the review-effort are different — a lose-`export` is a 1-character `git diff` (drop the `export` keyword) verifiable with `npx tsc --noEmit`, whereas a file deletion is multi-file (delete src + delete tests + audit transitive callers).
- **knip.json `entry` corrected to `server/vercel-entry.ts`.** The RESEARCH §Focus Area 1 skeleton listed `server/vercel.ts` which does not exist on this tree (Phase 14's `createApp()` factory in `server/app.ts` + `server/vercel-entry.ts` is the live entry). This was a Rule-1 fix-on-the-fly during Task 1 — captured in the Task 1 commit body and documented here so future re-runs of the RESEARCH skeleton catch the same drift.
- **Triage finding A propagated to W5 PLAN before W3 close.** The orchestrator amended W5 plan at SHA `bd5bbce` to add `VITE_POLL_WEATHER_MS` (default 1_800_000 = 30min) to the D-12 env-var introduction set. W3's contribution was the discovery + the `EXCLUDED — W5` annotation in the Hardcode-Target Manifest; the actual plan edit was an orchestrator commit, not a W3 commit. Recorded here so the cross-reference is durable.
- **Five Surprising Findings flagged for explicit user attention** before W4 begins (TRIAGE §"Surprising Findings"): (1) server-side `disperseEvents` is dead since the client-side migration; (2) `CountersSlot.tsx` carries a literal DEPRECATED comment; (3) `*Slot` wrapper components are test-only callers; (4) the 8th polling hook found and propagated to W5; (5) all 92 unused-type-exports landed in `ambiguous` per Pitfall 4. The user approved these on 2026-05-02 ('yes, add it. lets go'); the approvals are now baked into the W4 entry contract.
