Empty DLQ = no batches were timed-out = `watchdogTimeoutCount` was 0 throughout the run.

This satisfies D-08 ("Pre-tuning baseline must complete with `watchdogTimeoutCount = 0`") inferentially. From the next Wave 2 production run onward (post-Plan-06 commit `45a5dc4`), the field will appear directly in `events:llm-summary:v2` because `buildSummary()` now threads it.
