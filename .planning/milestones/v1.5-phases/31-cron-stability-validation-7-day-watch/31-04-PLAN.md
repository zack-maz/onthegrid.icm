PASS day requires ALL of:

- `/api/health endpoints.llmEvents.status === 'healthy'`
- Every DLQ reason in `events:llm-dlq` is in `WATCH_DLQ_WHITELIST = ['v3:timeout_watchdog', 'v3:adaptive-retry-fail']`
- `natural: true` (operator did NOT pass `--force`)

FAIL day = ANY of:

- `healthStatus !== 'healthy'`
- Any DLQ reason NOT in `WATCH_DLQ_WHITELIST`

GAP day = `healthStatus === 'unknown' && freshnessMs === null` (Redis lost the prior tick)

Per CONTEXT D-04: FAIL resets counter to 0; root cause documented in row `notes`. Watch restarts after cause addressed.
Per CONTEXT D-11: GAP rows DO NOT advance the counter, but they also DO NOT fail it — the counter PAUSES.

Per CONTEXT D-05: 3 reset cycles → escalate to Phase 31.1 limiter rework. Plan 05 covers escalation.
