<objective>
Eliminate the watchdog soft-warn tier entirely from `withBatchWatchdog` and its consumers (CONTEXT D-05, SIMPLIFY-03). Hard-kill stays as the single tier; its default value is unchanged in this plan (Plan 05 raises it from the Run-1 measurement). The synthetic callHistory row writer that emitted `skipReason: 'watchdog-soft-warn'` is deleted, and the enum value is dropped from both `LLMPipelineProgress` and `LLMRunSummary` row types. Soft-warn test cases are deleted atomically (RESEARCH Pitfall 5 — orphan tests on deleted code surface).

Why this is its own plan: D-05's deletion surface is the watchdog module + two callsites + the schema enum + the test file. Bundling with D-04 (Plan 03 / SIMPLIFY-01) would conflate two distinct simplification rationales and make the commit message harder to triage. CONTEXT D-08 explicitly notes commits 4 and 5 may swap order; this plan runs in parallel with Plan 05 (D-07 batch-size promotion) since their `files_modified` sets are disjoint at the line level (both touch `llmEventExtractor.v3.ts` but at different line ranges and conceptually different concerns — verify no merge conflict before commit).

Purpose: Pro 800s ceiling makes a 60s soft-warn at p50 ~27s batch latency mostly noise; Cerebras-running-slow signal is gone post-Phase-29 (D-01). Soft-warn data is derivable post-run from the analyzer's latency histogram (Plan 01's `retryAfterMs` + `latencyHistogram` in LLMRunSummary). Aligns with SIMPLIFY-\* philosophy: retire Hobby-era tiers, don't relax them.
</objective>
