## Honest shortfalls in Iteration 4

1. **Yemen-interior strikes NOT admitted.** The plan requested ~4-5 Yemen events including "US strikes on Houthi command/launcher sites (Sana'a, Saada, Hudaydah)" and "Israeli strikes on Hudaydah port / fuel depot." English-language reporting on Yemen-interior strikes in the Feb 28 - Apr 21, 2026 window is sparse — the major Sana'a / Hudaydah airstrike campaigns were in 2024 and 2025 (Israeli strikes on Sanaa airport May 2025; US Operation Rough Rider March-May 2025). CENTCOM press releases reference ongoing strikes on Houthi UAVs / anti-ship missiles / air-defense systems in "Houthi-controlled areas of Yemen" but without named-location specificity that passes the admission bar. Rather than fabricate a Sana'a neighborhood or a Hudaydah coordinate pair, Iteration 4 admitted only the 4 Houthi-on-Israel strikes (well-sourced to Wikipedia + Al Jazeera + LWJ).
2. **Syria theater short by 1-2 events.** Target was 3-4; delivered 2. Root cause: the canonical 2012-2024 pattern of Israeli strikes on Iranian assets in Damascus / Aleppo / T-4 simply does NOT appear in the 2026 Iran-war window per Wikipedia's dedicated Syria-in-2026-Iran-war and 2026-strikes-on-Syria pages. The Syria theater was dominated by Iraqi-militia-on-US-bases-in-Syria attacks (Kharab al-Jir Mar 23, Al-Tanf Mar 28, Qasrok Mar 29, T-4 Tiyas Mar 30) with the Israel-on-Syria axis limited to shelling of Daraa/Izraa tied to Druze protection. The two Syria entries admitted (gt-047 T-4 Tiyas, gt-048 Kharab al-Jir) are the best-sourced of the militia-on-US-Syria-bases pattern.
3. **No Ain al-Asad strike admitted.** Per Small Wars Journal / Fox News / Militarnyi, US forces completed withdrawal from Ain al-Asad Airbase in mid-January 2026 before the war began. Ain al-Asad was therefore NOT a US-hosting target during the war window and no February 28 - April 21 strike on it is well-documented. Iraq-theater strikes instead concentrate on Harir (gt-043) and Habbaniyah (gt-041), which remained US-relevant.

## Statistical framing

- **n = 50** ground-truth events
- **At p = 0.8** (resolver accuracy target), normal-approximation 95% CI = **±0.80 × 0.20 / √50 × 1.96 = ±11.1 pp**, narrowing from ±13.2 pp at n = 35.
- **At p = 0.8**, Wilson-score 95% CI ≈ **[0.67, 0.89]** (±11 pp), narrowing from [0.63, 0.91] at n = 35.
- **At p = 0.9** (stretch target), normal-approximation 95% CI = **±8.3 pp**.
- **At p = 0.95** (aspirational), normal-approximation 95% CI = **±6.0 pp**.
- **Plan D-25 gate:** prod-flip decision should require the point estimate of resolver accuracy to clear a stated threshold by at least the 95% CI half-width. With n = 50, a resolver that scores 85%+ would demonstrate the 80% floor with a conservative margin.

(The plan's checker iteration-1 note targeted "±8pp CI at p = 0.8" — that requires **n ≈ 96**; we deliberately did not pad to that level because the drop-rather-than-fabricate rule applies. The actual ±11 pp CI at n = 50 is still substantially tighter than the ±13.2 pp at n = 35 and is the best achievable without lowering the sourcing bar.)
