<objective>
Gather the GHOST-09 / SC-3 evidence with PRODUCTION data BEFORE the 403-demotion decision is implemented (D-14, mirroring Phase 42's diagnosis-first D-03 pivot pattern). Pull recent `prune-dead-urls` audit entries from the production `operator:audit-log` (the `prunedIds` sample), re-probe up to ~20 of those URLs with a browser-like User-Agent, AND re-probe the current prod `403`-status keys with a browser-like UA to test the operator-stated hypothesis ("bot-blocking CDNs 403 unknown UAs on live articles"). Record all verdicts and the resulting demote-or-keep decision in `43-VERIFICATION.md`. The recorded decision is then consumed by Plan 43-05's one-line prune-filter change.

Purpose: The asymmetric error budget makes the 403 decision evidence-driven, not assumed. If 403'd URLs serve live articles in a browser context, the bot-blocking-CDN false-positive hypothesis is confirmed → demote 403 to manual-only cron prune (expected outcome, D-14). If they 403 in a browser too, they are genuinely dead → keep cron-prunable. Either way the decision and its evidence are written down. SC-3 also requires confirming zero live events were swept by recent prunes.

Output: `scripts/sample-pruned-urls.ts` (or a documented one-off) + a recorded evidence section in `43-VERIFICATION.md` with the demote-or-keep decision.

NOTE: This plan is `autonomous: false` — it reads production data behind the operator Bearer (`DASHBOARD_PASSWORD`) and re-probes live publisher URLs. A checkpoint pauses for the operator to supply the Bearer / confirm prod access and to review the recorded verdicts before the decision is locked.
</objective>
